The Standard02 // 09

The Standard

Most golf gear looks the same. Most golfers arrive looking the same. Signum celebrates people, not products.

I

The Mark

A name, a number, an emblem — designed one-on-one with our studio and carried across every piece you commission. Not a logo you rent. A mark you own.

II

The Craft

Full-grain leathers, tournament-weight cloth, polished hardware. Materials chosen to take a patina and hold a memory — made to outlast the season, and the next.

III

The Meaning

Custom golf goods were once the quiet privilege of a few clubs. Signum opens the atelier to anyone who has earned their own mark — no membership required.
