# SIGNUM — Photography Brief & Image Generation Prompts

The website is deliberately built **design-system-first**: every image slot is currently an
art-directed material treatment (embossed marks, leather tiles, colorway swatches), so the site
looks intentional today and gets *better* — not rebuilt — when real photography drops in.

This document is the complete shot list. Each slot has:

- **Art direction** — for a photographer or product shoot
- **Prompt** — ready to paste into ChatGPT (DALL-E), Midjourney, or Adobe Firefly

---

## The Standard (applies to every image)

The test for every image: **"Would Hermès use this?"** If not, reject it.

**Always:** soft natural window light or early-morning course light · ivory/parchment/stone
backgrounds · one hero object or one human moment per frame · visible grain, stitching, brass
detail · calm negative space · editorial fashion sensibility.

**Never:** dark moody whiskey-bar lighting · black backgrounds · repetitive brown leather piles ·
stock-photo golf swings · logos of real brands · busy pro-shop clutter · plastic-looking AI sheen.

**Image mix across the site:** 60% lifestyle · 20% craftsmanship · 20% product.

**Colorways to art-direct around:** Parchment & Saddle · Racing Green & Brass · Navy & Champagne ·
Cream & Rose Gold · Espresso & Gold.

**Negative prompt (append to any generator that supports it):**
`no text, no watermarks, no brand logos, no dark moody bar lighting, no black background, not cluttered, no plastic sheen, no oversaturation`

## Priorities if you only commission three shots

1. **Hero still life** (slot 01) — carries the whole first impression.
2. **The Full Commission flat-lay** (slot 05f) — proves the one-mark-every-piece concept visually.
3. **One human lifestyle frame** (slot 02 or 06) — the brief demands 60% lifestyle; one great
   human image changes the emotional temperature of the entire site.

## Reshoot brief (from the Claude Design revision)

Current collection images are placeholders. Shoot each piece **in use, on a person**, in bright
natural light — a hand pulling the headcover, a towel over a shoulder mid-round, the shirt
shoulder turning through a swing. No seamless backdrops, no flat lays, no rendered surfaces.

**Casting:** the six frames should not read as one demographic. Across the collection grid and
chapter openers, cast Black, Latino, South Asian and East Asian players alongside white ones,
a range of ages from junior to seventy, and both women and men — the positioning is golfers
beyond the private club, and the photography has to say that before the copy does.
